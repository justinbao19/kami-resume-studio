Workflow

From raw material to an application-ready resume, with no wasted steps.

Work by hand, or let the Agent consolidate sources, ask the right questions, match the role, and write the first draft.

  1. 01

    Bring what you have

    Upload an old CV, or grant the Agent access to LinkedIn and the major Chinese recruiting platforms.

  2. 02

    Recover recent work

    The Agent asks about the recent role missing from your CV: scope, methods, metrics, outcomes, and true ownership.

  3. 03

    Match role and template

    Weigh your most defensible evidence against the target JD, then set language, content order, template, and theme.

  4. 04

    Edit, check, export

    Refine every line in the visual editor, preview either language, and export an A4 PDF with working links.

Agent workflow

It doesn't just reword your old resume. It gets to know your career first.

The Agent merges sources, flags title or timeline conflicts, and asks about the most valuable parts of your recent work that never made it onto paper. Every metric and claim needs a source or your confirmation.

  • Cross-platform source consolidation into one profile
  • Evidence coverage and keyword fit against the target job
  • A primary design, matching cover letter, and ATS-safe companion
  • No invented metrics and no inflated ownership
