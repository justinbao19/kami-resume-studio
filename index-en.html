Workflow

From source material to an application-ready resume, without unnecessary steps.

Work entirely by hand, or let the Agent handle source consolidation, evidence questions, role matching, and the first draft.

  1. 01

    Bring your starting point

    Upload an old CV or share career pages you have authorized the Agent to read, including LinkedIn and major Chinese recruiting platforms.

  2. 02

    Recover recent work

    The Agent asks about the latest role missing from your CV: scope, methods, metrics, outcomes, and true ownership.

  3. 03

    Match the role and template

    Use defensible evidence from your history to match the target job, then choose language, content order, template, and theme.

  4. 04

    Edit, check, and export

    Refine every line in the visual editor, preview Chinese or English output, and export an A4 PDF with working links.

Agent generation

It does not simply paraphrase your old resume. It learns your actual career story first.

The Agent merges sources, flags title or timeline conflicts, and asks for the most valuable parts of your latest work that were never written down. Every metric, responsibility, and outcome must have a source or your confirmation.

  • Cross-platform source normalization and candidate profile
  • Evidence coverage and keyword matching against the target job
  • A primary design plus an ATS-safe companion
  • No invented metrics and no inflated ownership
