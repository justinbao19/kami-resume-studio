# Resume Interview Playbook

Use this reference after source analysis. Ask only questions that can materially change positioning, evidence, or copy.

## Interview sequence

### Batch 1: latest role reconstruction

Ask 4-6 questions covering:

1. What outcome or business area did the role own?
2. What were the three recurring responsibilities or decisions?
3. What was the scale: users, customers, revenue, traffic, team, geography, budget, SKUs, tickets, or production volume?
4. What changed from before to after the user's work?
5. Which mechanisms or methods caused the change?
6. What did the user personally own versus contribute to?

### Batch 2: evidence and differentiation

Ask only after reading Batch 1 answers:

- Strongest metric and how it was measured.
- Hardest tradeoff, failure, incident, or constraint.
- Cross-functional or management scope.
- A representative project with action, scope, result, and business outcome.
- What peers in the same title usually did that this user did differently.

### Batch 3: target-role fit

Ask only for target-job requirements not yet supported:

- Direct evidence for a high-priority responsibility.
- Domain transfer evidence.
- Seniority evidence: strategy, architecture, hiring, budget, stakeholder influence, or ownership.
- Required tool or method experience.
- Language, location, work authorization, or certification when the target requires it.

## Question construction

Good question:

> 你提到负责知识库产品。上线前后，答案准确率、人工处理量或客户续费中，哪一个指标变化最明显？请给出大致基线、结果、统计周期，以及你做了什么导致变化。

Weak question:

> 你的工作成果是什么？

Each question should name the evidence gap and suggest relevant measurement dimensions without suggesting a flattering answer.

## Metric recovery prompts

When the user says "没有数据", ask for defensible proxies:

- Volume: users, clients, orders, tickets, stores, projects, pages, campaigns.
- Time: cycle time, response time, launch time, onboarding time, downtime.
- Quality: pass rate, defect rate, complaint rate, accuracy, conversion, retention.
- Money: revenue, renewal, budget, cost avoided, gross margin, deal size.
- Scope: team size, functions coordinated, regions, product lines, systems.
- Frequency: daily, weekly, monthly workload or release cadence.

Never fabricate precision. "约 30 家客户" is acceptable when the user confirms the estimate; `31.7` is not.

## Ownership calibration

Use the lowest truthful ownership verb:

- `owned / 方向负责人`: accountable for direction and outcome.
- `led / 主导`: independently drove the work to delivery.
- `coordinated / 牵头`: aligned multiple parties around a bounded result.
- `built / 负责`: owned a module or workstream.
- `contributed / 共建`: delivered a distinct part of shared work.
- `implemented / 参与`: executed without broader ownership.

If several people claim the same project, ask for the user's distinct module, decision, and metric.

## Stop conditions

The interview is complete when:

- Latest role has at least two evidence-rich bullets.
- At least half of selected bullets carry a number, defensible scale, or before/after statement.
- Target role's top three responsibilities each map to evidence or a clearly marked gap.
- Dates and titles have no unresolved material conflicts.
- The user has approved ownership wording for high-claim bullets.

If the user wants to proceed early, list unresolved claims and omit them from the resume.
